About Our School

Vignan group of schools is a great school established by the great man Dr. Pagidala Jalendhar Reddy and Pagidala Purendhar Reddy.

We offer classes from Nursery to 10th standard and provide a learning environment supported by academic assessments, practical facilities and foundation preparation.

  • Classes from Nursery to 10th standard
  • Monthly school examinations
  • Weekly IIT and NEET Foundation exams
  • Computer lab, playground and chemical lab
