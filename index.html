Vignan group of schools is a great school established by the great man Dr. Pagidala Jalendhar Reddy and Pagidala Purendhar Reddy.

  • Monthly school examinations
  • Weekly IIT and NEET Foundation exams

Learning Approach

Quality Education

At Vignan Group of Schools, we believe that every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and succeed. Our educational approach focuses on understanding concepts, developing problem-solving skills, and building a strong academic foundation. Through classroom learning, examinations, and practical activities, we encourage students to improve their knowledge and work towards their educational goals.

Regular practice, revision, and guidance help students identify what they understand well and what they need to work on. Students are encouraged to ask questions, participate in lessons, and develop confidence in their abilities.

Exams and Foundation Programs

Regular assessments and foundation preparation.

IIT and NEET Foundation Programme

Our IIT and NEET Foundation Programme is designed to introduce students to the fundamentals of science, mathematics, logical reasoning, and analytical thinking. Weekly foundation examinations provide opportunities for students to practise, assess their understanding, identify areas for improvement, and build confidence.

Monthly school exams Weekly IIT Foundation exams Weekly NEET Foundation exams

Contact the school for the current exam timetable, program details and class-wise information.

Importance of Examinations

Regular examinations help students review lessons, understand their progress, and strengthen their preparation. Monthly school examinations and weekly IIT/NEET Foundation examinations encourage consistent study habits and regular revision. Assessment results can also help students and teachers identify topics that need additional attention and support continued academic improvement.

Character Building

Discipline and Values

We believe that discipline, honesty, respect, punctuality, and responsibility are important parts of a student's development. We encourage students to respect their teachers and classmates, follow school rules, maintain good habits, and participate positively in school activities.

Our aim is to help students grow into responsible individuals who contribute positively to their families and society.

Learning Beyond Lessons

Modern Learning Facilities

Our school facilities include a computer lab, playground, and chemical lab to support different aspects of student learning. The computer lab provides opportunities to develop technology skills, the playground encourages physical activity and teamwork, and the chemical lab supports practical science learning.

These facilities complement classroom education and help students explore different areas of knowledge.

School and Family

A Message to Parents

Parents play an important role in a child's educational journey. A strong partnership between parents and teachers can support better learning habits, confidence, discipline, and personal development.

We encourage parents to communicate with the school, follow their child's academic progress, and motivate them to approach learning with curiosity, patience, and determination.
